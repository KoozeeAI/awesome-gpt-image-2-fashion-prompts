### No. 2: [Luxury Handbag Cinematic Product Commercial](./cases/luxury-handbag-cinematic-product-commercial.md)

#### 📖 Description

A curated AI fashion prompt for Handbag · Luxury handbag product commercial · Video.

#### 📝 Prompt

```
Ultra-Luxury Handbag Fashion Commercial
Style: Ultra-luxury fashion commercial, cinematic macro cinematography, glossy burgundy patent leather, premium studio lighting, shallow depth of field, elegant cinematic reflections, 4K UHD, photorealistic textures, sophisticated luxury aesthetic, slow and graceful camera movement, 24fps.

Scene 1 — Abstract Beginning | 0–3s

Extreme macro close-up of glossy burgundy patent leather flowing smoothly like liquid waves. Soft studio light slowly glides across the curved surface, creating refined highlights and cinematic reflections. The handbag remains completely unrecognizable at first, creating a sense of mystery, exclusivity, and luxury. Slow, elegant cinematic camera movement with extremely shallow depth of field.

Scene 2 — Crafted to Perfection | 3–6s

Transition into detailed macro shots showcasing the handbag’s exquisite craftsmanship. Focus on flawless stitching, perfectly polished leather edges, precise construction, and the refined strap attachment. Reveal premium gold hardware with subtle reflections and immaculate finishing. Slow focus transitions move naturally between each detail, emphasizing exceptional quality and craftsmanship.

Scene 3 — The Identity | 6–9s

A dramatic macro shot slowly reveals the iconic gold logo mounted elegantly on the rich burgundy leather. The camera gradually dollies closer as warm cinematic highlights sweep across the polished gold surface. The logo becomes the visual centerpiece, creating a powerful, sophisticated luxury reveal.

Scene 4 — The Reveal | 9–12s

The camera smoothly pulls back to reveal the complete handbag standing elegantly in a premium studio environment. A rich burgundy gradient background creates a sophisticated monochromatic atmosphere. A soft spotlight illuminates the handbag while a subtle glossy floor reflection enhances the premium presentation. Minimal composition, flawless product positioning, high-end editorial fashion aesthetic.

Scene 5 — Hero Ending | 12–15s

The camera performs a slow, elegant cinematic push-in toward the handbag. Dramatic studio lighting enhances the glossy burgundy leather, refined contours, and polished gold hardware. The surrounding background gradually becomes darker while the handbag remains beautifully illuminated as the hero product. End on a clean, sophisticated luxury fashion campaign frame with an ultra-premium advertising aesthetic.

Final Look: Ultra-realistic, photorealistic materials, flawless leather texture, premium gold reflections, cinematic lighting, controlled highlights, elegant shadows, luxury editorial composition, sophisticated color grading, smooth camera motion, no cuts, no distortion, no unnecessary objects, premium fashion advertisement quality.
```
